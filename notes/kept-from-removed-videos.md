# Kept from the removed videos

Useful facts, ideas and techniques saved from the weaker pieces (Union Berlin episode, the three Messi attempts) before their folders are removed from the repo. Everything stays recoverable in git history on branch `claude/barcelona-1925-animation-i9yjtx` (last commit before removal: `a35233a`).

## Union Berlin, "森林里的球场" (was labelled 第二集)

Verified story material, still good for a future episode:
- **2004 "Bluten für Union"**: club near losing its licence; fans gave blood and donated the payment to the club.
- **June 2008 – July 2009**: 2,300+ volunteers, 140,000 unpaid hours rebuilding and roofing the terraces (club stadium page).
- **Carols**: 2003, 89 fans climbed the gates and sang on the empty terraces; official since 2005, every 23 December, about 28,500 people, candles, *Stille Nacht*.
- **Since then**: first Bundesliga promotion 2019, Champions League 2023–24.
- Best line: 有的球场，是用钱买来的 / 这一座，是球迷亲手建起来的.

Technique worth reusing: a per-shot WebGL2 colour grade (bloom, halation, filmic shoulder, selective saturation keeping only reds, split toning, grain), with looks blended across dissolves.

## Messi ideas (messi-storm, messi-toon, messi-touch)

Story beats checked as facts:
- Lost finals: 2014 World Cup (0-1 Germany, a.e.t.), 2015 Copa América (Chile, 1-4 pens), 2016 Copa América (Chile, 2-4 pens; his penalty over the bar).
- After 2016 he said the national team was over for him (对我来说，国家队生涯结束了), then came back; 2021 Copa América and 2022 World Cup wins.
- Boyhood club in Rosario is **Newell's Old Boys** (red and black).

Concepts worth keeping:
- **Storm arc** (Su Shi 定风波 style): lone figure in rain, text as scenery, hits on the beat; fall (three finals) then return.
- **"The Touch" (最后一触)**: a 39-year-old No. 10 chases a ball to the touchline, each step goes back an era until a boy in Rosario is running; his last touch match-cuts to the boy, who is still playing. Closing line: *His story is told. The boy is still playing.*
- "Chronograph" look: light as the only material, one saturated colour per frame, hairline lines, Marey-style motion echoes.

Lessons from what didn't work:
- Rough cartoon Messi and the ink silhouette did not feel like Messi; the likeness has to come through first.
- Don't redraw other accounts' clips (messi-toon traced a Douyin clip from 抖音号 mingze006611).
- No crests, logos, real match photos or invented quotes.
